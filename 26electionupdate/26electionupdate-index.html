Campaign season update

Getting the progressive field AI-ready for 2026 and beyond

This campaign season, Higher Ground Institute (HGI) set out to get the progressive ecosystem AI-ready before Election Day by building the skills, use cases, and shared infrastructure the field needs to adopt AI on its own terms.

What we are running now

Fall AI Training Series (sign up for all sessions)

  • Weekly trainings across four job-based tracks - GEO, AI for Volunteer Engagement, AI for GOTV, and AI for Comms - built on the finding that role-specific training beats generic tool literacy
  • Claude 101 sessions hosted every Tuesday for baseline tool fluency
  • Weekly AI Office Hours where anyone can sign up for a 20 minute session with an expert to troubleshoot or get advice on AI in their campaign
  • Claude Code Study Halls: series running through December that helps non-technical organizers build their first replicable AI skills

Community and convening

  • Monthly AI Open Mics, the field's recurring hub for practitioners to learn about applied AI, hosted with Analyst Institute and the Research Collaborative - each averages 183 attendees
  • Co-hosting an AI + Cybersecurity working group with the DNC for progressive technology infrastructure and vendors
  • Launching soon: AI for Organizing Prize - cash prizes for the best ideas from the community on using AI in the work

Resources

  • AI Campaign Stack, a live directory of vetted AI tools and playbooks and guides for progressive campaigns
  • Launching soon: Progressive Tech Swap, an initiative to capture the code base for custom tools built for individual campaigns before they disappear

The reach

Since January, our AI Open Mic has drawn steady rooms and a growing core community.

  • 46 presenters from 38 organizations have taken the mic across this year's eight sessions
  • 183 average live attendees per session
  • 28% of attendees have come to 2 or more sessions
  • By August, 49% of attendees are returning
  • The single largest segment in the room is advocacy and organizing groups, alongside political tech, digital agencies, and data and analytics shops
  • Asked how their AI use compares to six months ago, 72% of August registrants said they are using AI more, and 44% said a lot more

AI Campaign Stack: the site

Since launching in March, AI Campaign Stack has reached roughly 3,200 practitioners in 49 states and the District of Columbia, and weekday use has nearly tripled.

  • 54 visitors on an average weekday, up from 19 in the spring
  • Returning visitors have grown four-fold, from 5 a weekday to 20, and are growing faster than new visitors - people are not just finding the site, they are coming back to it
  • Two-thirds of visitors read a page all the way to the bottom, and the average visit holds attention for a full minute
  • Organizers opened 72 different tool pages across the directory, and the Fall AI Training Series page alone drew 499 visitors

The impact

For our fall AI training series, we measure confidence on a 1 to 5 scale before and after each session.

  • Average confidence shift of +1.3, from 2.3 to 3.6 on a 5-point scale per training
  • The training works hardest on the least confident: people who improved started at 2.0 on average, while those who held steady were already high
  • Asked what would build their confidence, 75% named job-specific use cases, above every other answer combined
