The Details

Who can apply, and what sharing means

The point of the prize is to get good workflows, skills, and tools into more hands.

Who can apply

Individuals, and c3 or c4 organizations.

Campaigns cannot submit directly, though campaign staff are welcome to submit in a personal capacity.

Sharing your work

By submitting, you agree to share your work openly so others can use and build on it. Materials like prompts and workflows are shared under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0), and code like apps and skills under the MIT License.

You keep ownership and full credit for what you built. If your submission includes third-party code or content, flag it on the form so we can make sure it is cleared to share. HGI may publish any and all submissions for sharing with the community.
