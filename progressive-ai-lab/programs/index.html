Programs

Hands-on programs that build real AI skills.

The Progressive AI Lab runs recurring programs that meet practitioners where they are, from first-time users to experienced builders. Every program is designed to turn interest in AI into working capability across the progressive movement.

AI Open Mics

Monthly virtual convenings where practitioners bring real, specific needs: voter targeting, message testing, agentic workflow automation, campaign data security, and AI applications for democracy and human rights.
