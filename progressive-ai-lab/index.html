Frequently Asked

What is the Progressive AI Lab?

Straight answers to the questions people ask most about the Lab, its work, and how to get involved.

What is the Progressive AI Lab?

The Progressive AI Lab is the hub for AI innovation across the Democratic and progressive movement. It fosters innovation, elevates research, and coordinates strategic priorities and best practices for the use of AI in politics and organizing. It is a program of the Higher Ground Institute, and it builds practical tools such as AI Campaign Stack, runs hands-on programs, and helps campaigns and organizations adopt AI responsibly.

Who runs the Progressive AI Lab?

The Progressive AI Lab is a program of the Higher Ground Institute, a joint initiative of Cooperative Impact Lab and Higher Ground Labs. The work began at Higher Ground Labs and now runs through the Higher Ground Institute, which launched in January 2025.

What is AI Campaign Stack?

AI Campaign Stack is the Progressive AI Lab's flagship resource: a community-powered directory of more than 50 vetted AI tools and workflows for organizers and organizations, spanning communications, fundraising, field, and research, plus guides, skills, and a beginner-friendly starting point.

How can I get involved with the Progressive AI Lab?

You can explore AI Campaign Stack, register for a monthly AI Open Mic, sign up for a Claude Code Study Hall, join the newsletter, or contact the Higher Ground Institute team directly. Movement leaders, technologists, funders, and strategic partners are all welcome. Visit our Get Involved page to start.

Where did the Progressive AI Lab start?

The Progressive AI Lab began at Higher Ground Labs, where it funded incubation grants, ran training and programming, and published research for the progressive ecosystem. Those materials, including the 2024 Progressive AI Lab Report, remain available on the Higher Ground Labs site. The work now continues through the Higher Ground Institute.
